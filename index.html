Nina Chagas moved from Brazil to Ireland at eighteen. She built her career in Limerick, graduated from the University of Limerick in 2020, and has spent more than fifteen years learning the Irish market from the inside.

She founded Old to Gold to do one thing well: acquire neglected properties, bring them back to a high standard, and hold them as long-term rental assets.

The portfolio now spans three properties in Limerick. The same discipline that finds value in a derelict house is what she brings to every investor and owner she works with.

Rent-to-Rent

A fixed rent every month.
None of the management.

Rent-to-Rent is simple. We lease your property directly from you on a medium- to long-term agreement and pay you a fixed rent every month. We then take full responsibility for tenants, maintenance and day-to-day management. You are paid the same amount whether the property is occupied or not — and you never take a call about a broken boiler.

How it works

01

The agreement

We agree a fixed monthly rent and a term — typically three to five years — in a written contract that follows Irish tenancy rules.

02

We prepare it

Furnishing, compliance and any work needed to bring the property up to standard are carried out at our cost.

03

We manage it

Finding tenants, collecting rent, repairs and inspections are all handled by us. You have one point of contact.

04

You get it back

At the end of the term the property is returned to you in the same condition or better, fully maintained throughout.

If you own a property

  • The same rent lands every month, including during void periods.
  • No letting fees, no tenant screening, no late-night repair calls.
  • Routine maintenance and wear and tear are covered by us.
  • A long contract with a company that renovates for a living — the property is looked after.
  • Ideal for owners living abroad, inheriting a house, or simply done with being a landlord.

If you want to invest

  • Cash flow without buying: the capital goes into the contract and the fit-out, not a mortgage.
  • The return comes from the spread between what is paid to the owner and what the property lets for.
  • The model scales — each new agreement is a new income stream, not a new mortgage.
  • It runs alongside the buy-and-refurbish portfolio, which is where our operating experience comes from.

Every arrangement is set out in a written agreement and operated in line with Irish residential tenancy law. Figures discussed are specific to each property — this page is information, not a financial promise.
