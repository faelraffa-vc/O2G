Three stages, one house at a time.

01

Acquire

Derelict and undervalued houses across Limerick, bought below the cost of what they become. Grant eligibility is checked before we bid.

02

Refurbish

Full strip and rebuild. New layouts, new services, new bathrooms and kitchens, and a BER rating that makes the house cheap to run.

03

Let and manage

Tenants found, rent collected, repairs handled. The house is held as a long-term asset, not flipped.

A fixed rent every month.
None of the management.

We lease your house from you on a long agreement and pay you the same amount every month. Tenants, repairs and compliance become our problem, not yours. You are paid whether the house is occupied or empty.

01

The agreement

A fixed monthly rent and a term, usually three to five years, written down and operated under Irish tenancy law.

02

We prepare it

Furnishing, compliance and any work needed to bring the house up to standard, at our cost.

03

We run it

Tenant sourcing, rent collection, repairs and inspections. One point of contact, which is us.

04

You get it back

At the end of the term the house returns to you in the same condition or better, maintained throughout.

If you own a house

  • The same rent arrives every month, including through empty periods.
  • No letting fees, no tenant screening, no calls at eleven at night.
  • Routine maintenance and normal wear are covered by us.
  • Built for owners living abroad, owners who inherited a house, and owners who have had enough.

If you want to invest

  • Cash flow without buying. The capital goes into the agreement and the fit-out, not a mortgage.
  • The return is the gap between what the owner is paid and what the house lets for.
  • It scales. Each new agreement is another income stream, not another debt.
  • It runs beside the refurbishment portfolio, which is where the operating experience comes from.

Every arrangement is written down and operated under Irish residential tenancy law. Figures are specific to each house. This page is information, not a promise of return.
