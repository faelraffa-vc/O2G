For property owners

A fixed rent every month.
None of the management.

We lease your house from you on a long agreement and pay you the same amount every month. Tenants, repairs and compliance become our problem. You are paid whether the house is occupied or empty.

How the model works

01

Property assessment

We inspect the property and discuss your objectives.

02

Agree the structure

The proposed rent, the responsibilities, the term and the other commercial conditions.

03

Prepare the property

Where required, we arrange refurbishment, repairs, furnishing and compliance works.

04

The agreement

The appropriate legal agreement is put in place between you and our business.

05

Tenanting

We market the property, run the viewings and select suitable tenants.

06

Ongoing management

Rent collection, tenant communication, maintenance and inspections.

07

You receive the agreed income

Paid according to the terms of the contract, whether the property is occupied or empty.

What we take on

The exact responsibilities are agreed with you and set out in the contract. In a full arrangement they cover everything below.

Preparing the property

Refurbishment and repairs, decoration and presentation, furniture where applicable, safety checks and certification, and making sure the property meets the applicable rental standards.

Rental assessment and marketing

Market rent assessment, professional photographs, advertising, handling enquiries and arranging viewings.

Tenant selection

Application forms, identification checks, references, income verification where appropriate, previous landlord references and interviews.

Tenancy documentation

Preparing the written agreement, agreeing the terms, collecting the deposit and first rent, giving tenants the required information, and completing RTB registration.

Rent and deposits

Collecting rent, monitoring payments, handling arrears, holding deposits and keeping the records. You never chase rent.

Maintenance and repairs

Tenants contact us, not you. We assess requests, arrange contractors, coordinate repairs, deal with urgent issues and watch for recurring problems.

Inspections

Regular inspections, with the required notice, to catch maintenance problems, damage and safety concerns before they get expensive.

Tenant communication

We are the point of contact for questions, access arrangements and everything else that comes up during the tenancy.

Compliance and records

Records for the tenancy, rent, deposits, maintenance, inspections, safety documentation and RTB registration. Where specialist legal or tax advice is needed, we say so and you use a qualified professional.

If you own a house

  • The same rent arrives every month, including through empty periods.
  • No letting fees, no tenant screening, no calls at eleven at night.
  • Routine maintenance and normal wear are covered by us.
  • Built for owners living abroad, owners who inherited a house, and owners who have had enough.

If you want to invest

  • Cash flow without buying. The capital goes into the agreement and the fit-out, not a mortgage.
  • The return is the gap between what the owner is paid and what the house lets for.
  • It scales. Each new agreement is another income stream, not another debt.
  • It runs beside the refurbishment portfolio, which is where the operating experience comes from.

In her own words

Thank you for coming
to my website.

You are welcome to look around and to ask me anything. I came from Brazil at the age of eighteen to try for a better life. I knew it would be hard to leave my family, my friends, my culture and everything I knew behind. But I also knew I was not like most people in my village. I wanted more, and I could see that if I stayed, my future would be limited to what my town believed was normal.

My first job in Ireland was customer service in three different languages at Digital River. I gained a great deal of experience there and my English improved. After four and a half years in customer service I decided to go back to education at the University of Limerick, where I completed a Bachelor of Arts in Criminal Justice. That degree changed my life. It taught me to communicate, to work with people, and to take the initiative when it is needed.

Between academic years I worked with a corporate events company, HIYA Events and Entertainment, on conferences and events from the first client meeting through to final production. It can be a pressured job and it is a rewarding one, and I also trained as an event MC and DJ. I did my placement at the HSE in UHL as a receptionist and administrator. I am now a Senior Administrator at the University of Limerick.

At the end of 2022 I took my first step into property investment, in Brazil, alongside my family. In 2024 I began my first property investment in Ireland. What started as a vision is now a growing portfolio, with more than two properties and a third currently being refurbished. From old to gold, one property, one project and one lesson at a time.

At Old to Gold I am committed to my legal and ethical responsibilities. I value my tenants and work to keep a good relationship with them, built on communication and transparency. This journey is only beginning, and I want to thank you for taking the time to look at my website.

Nina Chagas
